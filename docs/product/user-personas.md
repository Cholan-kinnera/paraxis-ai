# User Personas — Paraxis AI

> **Purpose**: Definitive behavioral archetypes, pain points, motivations, and system access levels for Paraxis AI.

---

## 1. Student / Resident (`Aarav Patel`)
- **Profile**: 2nd-year Engineering student living in Hostel Block B.
- **Pain Points**: Submits tickets for broken Wi-Fi or leaking taps that disappear into administrative voids. Has no visibility into whether anyone is working on it.
- **Key Needs**: Instant natural-language reporting on mobile, immediate feedback, transparent status tracking, ability to confirm resolution.
- **Access Level**: Student Portal (Create incident, view self-reported incidents, view campus public notices).

---

## 2. Faculty / Instructor (`Dr. Priya Sharma`)
- **Profile**: Associate Professor of Computer Science; teaches in Seminar Hall 3.
- **Pain Points**: Projector or AC failures right before a 100-student lecture derail classes; no fast emergency technician hotline.
- **Key Needs**: Rapid classroom issue escalation with priority routing, class session attendance verification.
- **Access Level**: Faculty Portal (Classroom priority reporting, class attendance management).

---

## 3. Parent / Guest (`Rajesh Patel`)
- **Profile**: Parent visiting campus or checking in on student welfare.
- **Pain Points**: Lack of transparency around safety alerts or hostel emergency protocols.
- **Key Needs**: Verified public announcements, emergency contact channels.
- **Access Level**: Public Guest Access (Emergency notices, visitor registration).

---

## 4. Hostel Warden (`Vikram Singh`)
- **Profile**: Senior Warden responsible for 800 students in Boys' Hostel 2.
- **Pain Points**: Overwhelmed by duplicate student complaints on WhatsApp, uncoordinated mess food wastage, difficulty tracking recurring dorm maintenance issues.
- **Key Needs**: Consolidated hostel operational dashboard, automated duplicate grouping, dining headcount forecasts.
- **Access Level**: Warden Dashboard (Hostel-wide incident queue, mess forecast review, safety alerts).

---

## 5. Maintenance Staff / Electrician (`Kishore Kumar`)
- **Profile**: Senior electrician on campus for 12 years.
- **Pain Points**: Dispatched with vague instructions ("Fix light in Block B") without knowing room number, ladder requirements, or past breaker trips.
- **Key Needs**: Clear task assignments with exact room/asset context, easy one-tap acknowledgment, ability to submit resolution photos.
- **Access Level**: Technician Mobile View (Assigned tasks, acknowledge, mark in-progress, complete).

---

## 6. IT Support Staff / Network Admin (`Neha Reddy`)
- **Profile**: Network engineer managing campus Wi-Fi infrastructure and access points.
- **Pain Points**: 50 students submit 50 individual tickets when a single switch fails; flooded inbox.
- **Key Needs**: Automated duplicate clustering linking multiple reports to a single Access Point asset, diagnostic history.
- **Access Level**: IT Department Queue (Asset management, task execution, diagnostic logs).

---

## 7. Safety & Security Officer (`Capt. Ananya Sen`)
- **Profile**: Head of Campus Safety and Emergency Services.
- **Pain Points**: Sensitive safety complaints (harassment, stalking) mixed in with public tickets; lack of secure anonymous whistleblower channels.
- **Key Needs**: Encrypted, restricted-access safety cases, whistleblower reporter masking, emergency mass notification broadcast.
- **Access Level**: Safety & Trust Console (Encrypted SafetyCase review, emergency broadcast).

---

## 8. Campus Administrator / Operations Director (`Col. Sanjeev Mehta`)
- **Profile**: Chief Operating Officer of the University.
- **Pain Points**: Blind to vendor SLA breaches, high recurring maintenance expenditures without root-cause clarity.
- **Key Needs**: High-level Command Center, SLA compliance metrics, operational memory pattern discovery (e.g., "Why did Block B have 4 network outages this month?").
- **Access Level**: Campus Command Center (Full campus operational telemetry, policy override).

---

## 9. Super Administrator (`Platform Engineer`)
- **Profile**: Central IT Architect managing the multi-tenant Paraxis SaaS deployment.
- **Pain Points**: Managing cross-campus tenant onboarding, schema upgrades, AI provider quotas.
- **Key Needs**: Global tenant provisioning, API key management, audit trail inspection, system health metrics.
- **Access Level**: Super Admin Console (Cross-tenant provisioning, system configurations, audit export).
