# Product Charter — Paraxis AI

> **Product Name**: Paraxis AI  
> **Brand Etymology**: Derived from the Greek *praxis* (action, practice, practical execution). Paraxis symbolizes intelligent operational coordination in action.  
> **Positioning**: *"The Intelligent Operational Layer for Modern Campuses."*  
> **Core Brand Promise**: *"See what is happening. Understand what matters. Coordinate what happens next."*

---

## 1. What Paraxis AI Is (and What It Is Not)

### Paraxis AI IS:
- **An Intelligent Operational Substrate**: Sits above messy, fragmented physical and administrative workflows.
- **A Cognitive Coordinator**: Transforms natural-language student complaints, IoT anomalies, and mess logs into structured operational action graphs.
- **An Active SLA & Escalation Engine**: Automatically verifies whether dispatches occurred and escalates bottlenecks before students suffer.
- **An Operational Memory System**: Retains cross-incident context to detect chronic infrastructure failures (e.g., recurring AP failures in Block B).

### Paraxis AI IS NOT:
- **Not another bloated College ERP**: We do not try to replace fee collection, grading ledgers, or transcript databases.
- **Not a dumb ticketing queue**: We do not create passive Jira/Zendesk tickets that gather dust in unassigned queues.
- **Not a conversational chatbot toy**: We do not offer generic "chat with campus rules PDF" widgets. Our AI directly coordinates operational remediation.

---

## 2. Core Pillars of Operational Intelligence

1. **AI Campus Issue & Maintenance** *(North-Star Vertical Slice)*: Natural-language reporting, automatic location/asset resolution, duplicate grouping, priority scoring, policy verification, and technician dispatch.
2. **Smart Attendance & Communication**: Attendance anomaly detection (e.g., sudden drop in Block A morning attendance), automated notification orchestration, and teacher-warden coordination.
3. **Smart Hostel & Mess Operations**: Headcount demand forecasting, kitchen preparation planning, and operational food wastage minimization.
4. **Safety, Emergency & Trust**: End-to-end confidential safety reporting (harassment, mental health, hazards), anonymous reporter masking, and emergency broadcast dispatch.
